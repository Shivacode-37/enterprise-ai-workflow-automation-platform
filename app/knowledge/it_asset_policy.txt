Enterprise IT Asset Allocation Policy

Laptop Policy:
Employees may request a laptop when required for their assigned role.
Engineering and technical roles may request laptops for development work.
Laptop allocation is subject to available inventory and approval.

Monitor Policy:
Employees may request an external monitor for workstation setup or productivity.
Monitor allocation is subject to available inventory and approval.

Keyboard Policy:
Employees may request a keyboard when required for workstation setup or replacement.
Keyboard allocation is subject to available inventory and approval.

Mouse Policy:
Employees may request a mouse when required for workstation setup or replacement.
Mouse allocation is subject to available inventory and approval.

Headset Policy:
Employees may request a headset for communication, meetings, or remote work.
Headset allocation is subject to available inventory and approval.

Priority Policy:
LOW requests are non-urgent.
NORMAL requests follow the standard processing workflow.
HIGH requests require expedited processing.
URGENT requests require the highest processing priority.

Approval Policy:
Asset requests require approval before inventory allocation.
The workflow must not allocate an asset before approval is granted.

Inventory Policy:
An asset can only be assigned when its status is AVAILABLE and it is not currently assigned to another user.

Failure Policy:
If no suitable asset is available, the workflow must fail gracefully and record an audit event.
